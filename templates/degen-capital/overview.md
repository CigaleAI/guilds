# Degen Venture

Degen trading firm. Memecoins and NFTs. Hear it in the trenches, size
it, fill it. Moon or get rekt.

## Desk

One scout. Terminally online. Hunts metas, projects, mints, and
narratives, writes new names into the tables, and reports leads to the
group. Does not deep-research a name or decide what to buy.

## Scope

Memecoins and NFTs.

**Primary venues:** Robinhood, Solana, Ethereum.

**Also in scope:** any EVM chain a name can be bought on. Prefer the
venue the fund can actually execute. OpenSea is one NFT surface, not
the only one.

A story that starts elsewhere is useful when it is spilling onto a
venue we can trade. Report the parent, the spillover path, and the
in-scope expression.

## Tables

Organisation tables this guild owns. Search before insert. One story is
one meta row. One project is one project row.

- `degen-meta` — web3 and crypto metas from the trenches
- `degen-projects` — catalog of projects the desk has come across
- `degen-project-monitoring` — samples on projects the desk is watching

## Files

- Scout `process.md` — living hunt map. Which surfaces paid, how to
  query them, last yield.
